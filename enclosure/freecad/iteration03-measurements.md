# Physical verification required before manufacturing iteration 03

No component dimensions below are established by the CAD placeholders. Record
actual part identity, method, units, uncertainty, date, and measured samples.
Keep sourced nominal dimensions separate from measured values and fit allowances.

| Assembly | Required measurements / verification |
| --- | --- |
| Each LCD | Actual PCB outline and thickness; hole coordinates and diameters; active-area diameter/position relative to PCB; glass/bezel height; rear components, solder, header and connector envelopes; wire exit; viewing cone |
| Lens stock | Actual diameter/thickness, tolerances, edge finish, flatness, grade and traceability; scratch resistance, optical quality, thermal expansion; validated impact/retention behavior |
| Lens pocket/retainer | Radial fit coupons for lens and nose; axial stack and compliance allowance; lens peripheral bearing width; ring-to-PCB gap; retainer attachment, tool access and assembly sequence; no active-area load under deformation |
| ESP32-S3 | Actual variant, PCB/headers/underside envelope, mounting provisions; USB connector location, plug envelope, insertion/removal access and cable bend radius |
| Breadboard assembly | Actual footprint, depth, rail breaks, adhesive backing if present, jumper/header height, assembled wiring bundle and strain relief; whether bench components can be relocated as proposed |
| Both encoders | Actual body/tabs/terminals and mounting interface; shaft diameter, flat profile, length above panel; axial motion and operating loads; achievable knob grip and attachment engagement |
| Seven switches | Body and contact spacing; actuator height, force and travel; release clearance and overtravel; cap guidance, anti-rotation, positive retention and structural load bypass |
| Controls | Actual hand access and accidental activation on adult-operated mockup; knob friction and cap finish; moving gaps, pinch/entrapment paths; pull/twist resistance of attachment designs |
| Wiring/power | Actual lead lengths, connector reach, bend radii, power/USB arrangement, cable pull loads, routing and service slack, supply and thermal behavior under operation |
| Rear service closure | Fastener/insert selection and measured geometry; wall engagement; tool clearance and disassembly access; supported board mounts; retention after repeated service cycles |
| Print process | Usable XYZ envelope including brim/keep-outs; material specification; XY/Z fit coupons, shrinkage/warping, layer adhesion, surface finish, support strategy and actual finished wall thickness |

Safety evaluation must additionally cover materials/finishes, electrical access,
small parts, impact and control retention, pinch points, edges, cable handling,
and foreseeable use. Qualified review determines applicable requirements and
test methods. This list is engineering preparation, not a completed safety
assessment or authorization for toddler handling.
